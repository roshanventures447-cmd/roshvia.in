THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

Before you find your favourite.

What comes in a bedsheet set?

This demo shows matching bedsheet and pillow-cover styling. Exact set contents, fabric specifications and measurements need to be confirmed for each product before the store opens.

How do I choose a size?

Measure your mattress width, length and depth, then allow enough extra fabric for tucking. Single, Double and King are illustrative options in this demo; final measurements will be added to the live catalogue.

How should I care for satin bedding?

Follow the wash-care label included with your actual product. Specific care instructions will be added when the final fabric and product specifications are confirmed.

Can I place an order here?

You can try the complete shopping flow: choose a colour and size, add it to your bag and preview checkout. This is a design demo, so no real orders or payments are taken.

What about delivery and returns?

Shipping areas, charges, timelines and return terms will be added before the store goes live. No delivery or refund commitment is made in this demo.
